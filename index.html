How We Inspect Before We Treat

For homes in Coeur d'Alene, we start with what you have seen, where the activity is, and what has changed. Then we check attics, garages, crawl spaces, foundations, doors, vents, utility openings, and landscaping.

The goal is to name the pest and find out why it is there. Mice need only a gap a little wider than a quarter inch, and one common way in is where pipes and wires enter the house. Carpenter ants are a serious building pest in northern Idaho, where moisture is plentiful, and their main nest is typically outdoors. So we look for damp wood and follow the trail back to the nest.

This follows the Integrated Pest Management steps the EPA describes: set an action threshold, monitor and identify the pest, prevent what attracts it, and use control methods when those steps are not enough. EPA notes that one pest does not always mean control is needed.

What pests are common in Coeur d'Alene?
Carpenter ants, mice, spiders, wasps, and hornets are common calls. University of Idaho Extension calls carpenter ants serious building pests in northern Idaho, where moisture is plentiful. In the Pacific Northwest, deer mice are the main carrier of hantavirus, so we take mice seriously.
How do you get rid of bald-faced hornets in Coeur d'Alene?
If the nest is away from people, you can leave it. NC State Extension says the colony dies out each year and the nest is not reused. Iowa State Extension says bald-faced hornets usually attack if someone comes within 3 feet of the nest. If it is near a door, deck, or walkway, or inside a wall, call us. We remove it by the product label.
How do I choose a pest control company in Coeur d'Alene?
Ask five things. Is the company licensed in Idaho? Will a technician inspect and identify the pest first? Will you get the price in writing? Does the plan include follow up and prevention, not only spraying? Is the guarantee in writing? The EPA's IPM guide also asks whether pest management staff are licensed and trained.
What does pest control cost in Coeur d'Alene?
Cost depends on the pest, the size of the property, and how bad the problem is. You get the price in writing before work starts. If you want affordable pest control, ask each company for a written price and compare what is included.
How do you prevent pests from coming back after treatment?
Fix what attracts them. The EPA's IPM guide says prevention means removing food, water, and shelter. That means reducing clutter, sealing gaps where pests get in, and clearing standing water. After each visit we tell you what to fix, and we recheck after treatment.
