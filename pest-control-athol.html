Common Pest Problems in Athol

Athol includes wooded properties, rural homes, outbuildings, and residential areas. These property conditions can create different pest concerns throughout the year.

Carpenter Ants Around Wood Structures

Carpenter ants may be found around decks, wood siding, logs, crawl spaces, and other areas where moisture or damaged wood is present.

Mice in Garages & Outbuildings

Garages, sheds, barns, wood piles, and other structures can provide rodents with shelter. Signs may include droppings, scratching sounds, nesting material, and gnaw marks.

Wasps & Hornets Around Homes

Wasps and hornets may build nests under roof edges, eaves, decks, sheds, trees, and other protected locations around a property.

Fleas & Ticks Around Outdoor Areas

Tall grass, wooded property edges, pets, and wildlife activity can contribute to flea and tick activity around homes and outdoor areas.
